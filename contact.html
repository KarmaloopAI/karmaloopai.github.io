Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What is Jiva and how can it benefit my business?

Jiva is an autonomous agent framework powered by LLMs that excels at solving complex tasks autonomously. It can benefit your business by automating repetitive tasks, providing intelligent decision support, and enabling new capabilities that were previously not possible with traditional automation tools.

How does Fluen improve code documentation?

Fluen is an automated code documentation solution that uses LLMs to generate comprehensive, accurate documentation for your codebase. It improves code documentation by reducing the manual effort required, ensuring consistency across your codebase, and making it easier for developers to understand and maintain code.

What industries do you typically work with?

We work with a wide range of industries, including but not limited to technology, finance, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, and professional services. Our AI solutions are adaptable to various business contexts and can be customized to meet the specific needs of different industries.

How do you approach data privacy and security?

We take data privacy and security very seriously. Our solutions are designed with privacy and security in mind, and we adhere to industry best practices and regulations. We work closely with our clients to ensure that their data is protected and that our solutions comply with their specific security requirements.

What is the typical implementation timeline for your solutions?

The implementation timeline varies depending on the complexity of the solution and the specific requirements of your business. Typically, our implementation process ranges from a few weeks for simpler solutions to several months for more complex, enterprise-wide implementations. We work closely with you to develop a realistic timeline and ensure a smooth implementation process.
